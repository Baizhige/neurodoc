## Paradigm

This dataset contains scalp EEG recordings from pediatric subjects with intractable seizures monitored at Boston Children's Hospital. Recordings were collected from 22 pediatric subjects (ages 1.5-22 years) with refractory epilepsy, plus one additional adult subject (chb24). Subjects were monitored for up to several days following withdrawal of anti-seizure medication to characterize their seizures and localize seizure foci for possible surgical intervention. The data includes continuous long-term EEG recordings with seizure events annotated by expert neurologists.
