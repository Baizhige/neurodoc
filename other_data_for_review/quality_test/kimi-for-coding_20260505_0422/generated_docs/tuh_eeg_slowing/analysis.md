## Paradigm

The TUH EEG Slowing Corpus is a clinical EEG dataset focused on the detection and characterization of slowing patterns in EEG recordings. The dataset comprises retrospective clinical EEGs collected from patients at Temple University Hospital, with annotations indicating the presence and type of slowing abnormalities. Recordings were acquired using standard 10-20 electrode placement systems with various channel configurations. The primary task involves identifying and classifying diffuse, focal, or polymorphic slowing patterns that are indicative of underlying neurological dysfunction.

## Data Quality Notes

- No event annotations were detected in the scanned files; the Event Structure table is empty.
- All demographic and equipment metadata fields (age, sex, handedness, amplifier, cap) are completely missing from the dataset.
- Channel nomenclature is highly heterogeneous with mixed reference types (LE, REF) and non-standard labels (LUC, RLC, SP1, SP2, PG1, PG2).
- Multiple DC channels (DC1-DC through DC8-DC) are present as non-EEG auxiliary channels.
- Special-purpose channels including BURSTS, IBI, PHOTIC, PHOTIC PH, and SUPPR suggest clinical monitoring protocols but their exact meanings are not documented in the scan metadata.
- The dataset appears to contain both standard 10-20 channels and extended channels (26-32), with variable coverage across recordings.
