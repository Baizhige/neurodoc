## Paradigm

The Dot Pattern Expectancy (DPX) task, a version of the Continuous Performance Task (AX-CPT), was used to assess proactive versus reactive cognitive control in sub-acute mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI) patients and healthy controls. Participants responded to cue-probe pairs (CueA/CueB × Probe aX/aY/bY), enabling decomposition of context-maintenance and response-inhibition processes via P3a/P3b ERP components. Data collected at 500 Hz with 65-channel EEG cap using standard DPX trial structure.
