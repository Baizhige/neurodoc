## Paradigm

Participants learned and performed specific rhythmic drumming patterns at varying tempos using keyboard keys, receiving adaptive visual feedback on timing accuracy. EEG was recorded continuously during drumming training. Different stimulus codes (S 1 through S 135) correspond to specific drum keys, tempos, or experimental conditions. Each key press generates a Stimulus event marker; the dataset contains over 65,000 drumming events across all participants.
