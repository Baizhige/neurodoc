## Paradigm

Resting-state EEG was recorded with eyes open and eyes closed before and after a two-hour block of cognitive tasks (working memory, attention, processing speed) across a lifespan cohort of adults. A 5-year longitudinal follow-up is included. BrainVision S-codes encode the specific resting-state condition (pre/post × eyes open/closed) and session number, with high-count events (~200 per event) representing primary condition markers and lower-count events representing session-specific or follow-up markers.
