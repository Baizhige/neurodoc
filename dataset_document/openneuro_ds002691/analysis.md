## Paradigm

A sustained internal attention task where participants were asked to either mentally concentrate on a target (concentrate condition) or relax (relax condition) for 15-second sustained blocks with eyes closed. Transitional cues marked preparation periods between active blocks. The study investigated EEG signatures of sustained internal attention versus relaxation using a binary classification design.
