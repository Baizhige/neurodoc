## Paradigm

Naturalistic listening task where older Cantonese-speaking adults (65+) listened to a Cantonese translation of "The Little Prince" divided into story sections, followed by periodic comprehension checks, during simultaneous EEG and fMRI recording. The story contains diverse linguistic content including clauses, function words, and content words at varying frequencies. Numeric trigger codes 1-112+ mark story segments and experimental conditions, with code 14 being most frequent (1,053 total occurrences) followed by code 15 (648), consistent with a mixed experimental design.
