## Initial corpus review

The eight trained reviewers represented in the released entry metadata are:

- Shu Peng

The initial cohort comprised one third-year undergraduate, four second-year Master's students, one research assistant, and two PhD students. Reviewers were recruited through targeted invitations based on relevant EEG-processing experience and completed three 30-minute training sessions.

Each released entry was reviewed by at least one human reviewer and one repository administrator. Review was not conducted as independent multiple ratings, and no inter-rater agreement statistic was collected. The `Manual Review` field in each released `analysis.md` records the reviewer attribution for that entry.

## Release authority

NeuroDoc produces rulebook-constrained candidates. Human reviewers and repository administrators retain authority over corrections and admission to the released corpus. Blocking validation errors cannot be overridden by a reviewer.
