## Paradigm

Participants performed dynamic decision-making tasks including food preference choices, semantic word judgments, and image categorization while high-density EEG (129 channels) and continuous mouse-tracking were recorded simultaneously. The study captures the real-time neural dynamics of binary choice formation, including evidence accumulation, conflict monitoring, and response selection. Numeric trigger codes in the 0200–0500 series encode stimulus presentation, trial phases, responses, and outcome information across three experimental paradigms.
